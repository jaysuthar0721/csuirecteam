Now Competing At IREC

We've moved from NASA USLI to the International Rocket Engineering Competition.

For the past several years, Ram Rocketry competed through Ram Launch Initiative (RLI), our NASA University Student Launch Initiative (USLI) senior capstone team — finishing 7th out of 67 teams in our debut year. We're proud of that history, and you can still read about that program on its official CSU page.

Ram Rocketry is now transitioning the club's competition focus to the International Rocket Engineering Competition (IREC), held in Midland, Texas — marking our first entry into IREC in over six years. It's a bigger stage, a fully student-owned vehicle, and the next step in proving what this team can build and fly.

What We Do

Hands-on rocketry, real aerospace engineering experience.

Ram Rocketry is a collegiate rocketry organization dedicated to firsthand learning through experimental rocketry. We welcome everyone — from seasoned high-power enthusiasts to students who are simply curious about space. Members earn High-Power Rocketry certifications and build genuine aerospace engineering experience across a range of ambitious projects.

Certification Program

We run High-Power Rocketry Level 1 & 2 certification flights all year, built around our own in-house, 3D-printable, flight-tested L1 kit.

Real Engineering Skills

MATLAB, Simulink, CAD (SolidWorks & Fusion 360), CFD, FEA, Python, and C++ — applied to full rocket design, build, and flight cycles.

Industry Networking

We host site visits and networking events with companies including Lockheed Martin, United Launch Alliance, Sierra Space, and Ursa Major.

Two-Year Mission Roadmap

From a competitive solid rocket to a student-built liquid engine.

Phase 1 · 2026–2027

IREC 2027 — 10,000 ft COTS Solid Rocket

A full, student-designed high-power rocket — airframe, nosecone, fins, dual-deploy recovery, and an airbrake system — flying on a commercial off-the-shelf solid motor toward a 10,000 ft target apogee at IREC in Midland, Texas.

Phase 2 · 2026–2028

Liquid Propulsion Engine Development & Static Fire

In parallel, our Liquid Propulsion team is designing, manufacturing, and hot-fire testing a student-built, pressure-fed bi-propellant engine (ethanol/N₂O) with a pintle injector and ablative cooling.

Phase 2 · 2028

IREC 2028 — Liquid-Propelled Rocket Flight

Integrating our validated engine into a flight vehicle and competing at IREC, targeting a 10,000 ft apogee — making Ram Rocketry one of very few collegiate teams flying a student-designed liquid engine internationally.
