03 / The road ahead

From the workshop
to the launch rail.

Two parallel programs, with every design review and test building toward flight.

2026–2027 / Solid rocket

IREC 2027

A 10,000 ft COTS campaign with a student-designed airframe, dual-deploy recovery, onboard avionics, and active airbrakes.

2026–2028 / Engine development

Build. Test. Static fire.

Design, manufacture, and hot-fire a student-built, pressure-fed liquid rocket engine.

2028 / Flight goal

Fly our own propulsion.

Integrate the validated engine into a flight vehicle targeting IREC 2028.
