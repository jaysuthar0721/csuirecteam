Engineering Projects

What we build year-round.

Alongside our year-round HPR certification program, Ram Rocketry runs an experimental rocketry track that tackles a new engineering challenge every year.

In Development

Active Fin Stabilization: "Long Shot"

An actively controlled rocket using servo-actuated fins and onboard avionics to demonstrate full 3-axis (roll, pitch, and yaw) control. It corrects attitude in flight and optimizes trajectory performance in real time.

This two-phase canard control demonstrator has directly sharpened our team's avionics and control-system capabilities. That work feeds straight into the design of our IREC 2027 competition vehicle.

Competition Build

Two-Stage Rocket

For the first time in club history, Ram Rocketry is competing in a two-stage rocket challenge: carry the maximum number of USGA-approved golf balls to the maximum altitude possible, while staying under a specific total motor impulse.

Scoring is calculated with a non-linear equation that balances altitude against payload mass, which pushes the team to optimize staging, mass budgeting, and motor selection all at once.

In Development

Glider Rocket

A rocket that transitions mid-flight into a remotely controlled glider. The vehicle uses fixed wings and onboard avionics to achieve controlled, winged recovery instead of a traditional parachute descent.

It's an ambitious departure from standard dual-deploy recovery, and a proving ground for flight-control software and structural design under aerodynamic loads.
