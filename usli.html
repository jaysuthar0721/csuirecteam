Program History

Our roots: NASA's University Student Launch Initiative.

Before IREC, Ram Rocketry spent two years of competition in NASA's University Student Launch Initiative (USLI) through Ram Launch Initiative (RLI), our senior capstone team.

Ram Launch Initiative

How RLI worked.

For several years, Ram Rocketry's competition arm was Ram Launch Initiative (RLI), our senior-capstone team in NASA's University Student Launch Initiative. RLI was one of the first Colorado university teams to compete in that program.

Over two years of competition, RLI students designed and flew a full high-power rocket to NASA standards, finishing 7th out of 67 teams in our debut year.

RLI's core team was made up of graduating seniors, with underclassmen from Ram Rocketry volunteering as interns, which made our club the feeder program for that competition team. That structure gave the club a strong foundation in full-vehicle design, review milestones, and formal technical reporting.

Quick Facts

  • Program: Ram Launch Initiative (RLI)
  • Competition: NASA University Student Launch Initiative (USLI)
  • Active: 2022 – 2025
  • Debut result: 7th of 67 teams
  • Team structure: all-senior core team, with underclassman interns
Where We Are Now

Our current competition focus is IREC.

Ram Rocketry has since shifted its primary competition focus to the International Rocket Engineering Competition (IREC), held in Midland, Texas. For CSU's official record of the USLI program, Ram Launch Initiative's page above is the best source.
