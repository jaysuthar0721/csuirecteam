Liquid Propulsion Project

Building CSU's first student-led liquid rocket engine.

A bi-propellant, pressure-fed liquid engine, designed, built, and tested entirely by Ram Rocketry students, targeting a flight at IREC 2028.

Why We're Building It

Rigorous engineering, safe testing, real industry workflows.

We're building the first student-led liquid rocket engine program at Colorado State University, with a focus on rigorous engineering, safe testing, and design workflows that mirror industry practice.

Targeting a 10,000 ft apogee on a liquid engine would make Ram Rocketry one of very few collegiate teams to fly a student-designed liquid engine at an international competition.

This program runs alongside our IREC 2027 solid rocket campaign, not in place of it. Together they're the two competition tracks Ram Rocketry is running right now.

At a Glance

  • Bi-propellant, pressure-fed architecture
  • 80% ethanol / 20% water fuel, nitrous oxide oxidizer
  • Target thrust: ~1,100 N
  • Timeline: design and testing through 2026–2027
  • Goal: flight vehicle at IREC 2028
Development Roadmap

From test stand to flight vehicle.

2026 – 2027

Engine Development & Static Fire

Our Liquid Propulsion team is designing, manufacturing, and hot-fire testing a student-built, pressure-fed bi-propellant engine with a pintle injector and ablative cooling.

2028

IREC 2028: Liquid-Propelled Rocket Flight

Integrating our validated engine into a flight vehicle and competing at IREC, targeting a 10,000 ft apogee on a fully student-designed liquid engine.

Engine Specs

The current design baseline.

Our goal is simple: learn fast, build the engine correctly, and execute safely. Here's the current design baseline, open to iteration as testing progresses.

ParameterSpecification
ArchitecturePressure-fed, bi-propellant
Fuel80% Ethanol (C₂H₅OH) / 20% Water
OxidizerNitrous Oxide (N₂O)
InjectorPintle injector
CoolingAblative thermal protection
ManufacturingCNC-machined stainless steel, manufacturable in university machine shops
Target Thrust~1,100 N
MilestoneStatic fire & hot-fire test campaign, 2026–2027
