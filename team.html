Our Structure

A club built by and for its members.

Leadership oversees project teams spanning structures, avionics, propulsion, and recovery, alongside dedicated finance, fundraising, and outreach roles that keep the club funded and connected to the aerospace industry.

Most members join with limited experience and leave with the technical skills, certifications, and project experience needed to excel in the aerospace industry.

Interested in a leadership role?

Ram Rocketry is always looking for motivated students to help lead project teams, manage sponsorships, or run outreach and events.
