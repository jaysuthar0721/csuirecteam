About Ram Rocketry

Ambitious thinkers, real engineering challenges.

A collegiate rocketry organization at Colorado State University that learns rocketry by building it.

Our Mission

Igniting student interest in rocketry and aerospace.

Ram Rocketry is a collegiate rocketry organization at Colorado State University built around learning by doing. We welcome everyone, from seasoned enthusiasts to those simply curious about space. Our members earn high-power rocketry certifications and build real aerospace engineering experience across a range of ambitious projects.

Our mission is to get students excited about rocketry and aerospace. We're a community of ambitious thinkers taking on real engineering challenges, and we're just getting started.

What Membership Looks Like

  • High-Power Rocketry Level 1 & 2 certification flights held throughout the year
  • An in-house, 3D-printable, flight-tested L1 certification kit built by the team
  • Hands-on design, build, and test cycles on active rocket projects
  • Technical skill-building in MATLAB, Simulink, CAD, CFD, FEA, Python, and C++
  • Direct networking with aerospace industry professionals and companies
Two Ways We Grow

Technical rocketry knowledge, meets industry experience.

Technical Rocketry Knowledge

Our certification track runs year-round and gives members a clear path from first build to HPR Level 1 & 2. Alongside that, our experimental rocketry program rotates through a new engineering challenge every year, from active flight control to two-stage design to full liquid propulsion.

Aerospace Industry Networking

We've hosted networking events and site visits with companies including Lockheed Martin, United Launch Alliance, Sierra Space, Ursa Major, and Boeing. That gives members real exposure to the aerospace industry long before graduation.

Our Programs

Three programs, one team.

2022 – 2025

Ram Launch Initiative (NASA USLI)

Two years competing in NASA's University Student Launch Initiative, finishing 7th out of 67 teams in our debut year. Our roots as a competition team.

2026 – Present

IREC

Ram Rocketry's current competition focus: the International Rocket Engineering Competition (IREC), held in Midland, Texas, with a fully student-owned vehicle program.

2026 – 2028

Liquid Propulsion Project

Building Colorado State University's first student-led liquid rocket engine, a bi-propellant design targeting a flight vehicle at IREC 2028.
